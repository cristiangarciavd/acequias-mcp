# Similar games and novelty of Acequias - research notes

Research for #170: existing board and card games with themes or mechanics close to
Acequias (Rules v3: river strip, neutral canal pieces, vineyards planted on hex
corners and split by thirds, floodgate bribes from the action die, varietal
roulette, bodegas), with a specific search for games made in Mendoza or Argentina.

Other games are named **only in this note**. Their rules, names and art must not be
copied into Acequias.

Searches were made in November 2026. BoardGameGeek refused automated access (HTTP
403 on pages, "Unauthorized" on its XML API), so its database could not be searched
directly; facts below come from rulebooks, publisher pages, Wikipedia and press.
A game that is only on BoardGameGeek could have been missed (see
[Limits](#limits-and-uncertain-facts)).

## Closeness legend

- **Theme**: water / irrigation, wine / vineyards, or Mendoza / Argentina
- **Mechanics**: a shared network where water or flow decides scoring, points split
  among neighbours, or sabotage of the network
- **Both**: theme and mechanics

## Mendoza and Argentina

Searched: local press (Diario Uno, Unidiversidad), the Mendoza government (press and
its social-economy catalogue), the Departamento General de Irrigación (DGI), INTA,
and Mendoza publishers. **No commercial game about acequias, irrigation, wine or the
*vendimia* made in Mendoza was found.**

- The DGI's *Aquaderno* kit for 4- and 5-year-olds includes a water board game,
  *Guardianes del Agua*, about caring for water (30 September 2015)
  ([Mendoza gov., Aquaderno ya llegó a 100 salitas, 2015][dgi]). Educational, not a
  strategy game; **Theme** only.
- INTA's *Aguados* (published on educ.ar, 25 March 2020) is a board game "para que
  pruebes cuánto sabés sobre el agua", for primary school
  ([educ.ar, Aguados, 2020][aguados]). A quiz-style educational game; **Theme**.
- Mendoza publisher MendoZen (founded 2015) makes games on local themes: the Zonda
  wind, the Crossing of the Andes, the Huarpe people
  ([Diario Uno, 12 Sep 2018][mendozen]; [Unidiversidad, 1 Nov 2021][unidiversidad]).
  Neptuno Games (the publishing arm of Mendoza's Aquí Hay Dragones) and other local
  authors are listed in [Diario Uno, 18 Jan 2026][diariouno2026]; none of their
  games is described as being about wine, water or acequias.
- *Vinotipia* (Córdoba, 2025) is a guided blind-tasting game with emotion cards,
  for 2 to 6 players ([InfoNegocios, 4 Sep 2025][vinotipia]). **Theme** (wine) only.
- *Winemakers* (Buenos Aires, 2006) is in the wine table above.

## Name check: "Acequias"

- No board, card or video game called "Acequias" or "Acequia" was found in web
  searches. BoardGameGeek could not be searched directly (see above).
- Similar names, not clashes:
  - *Walk Along the Acequia*: a free Twine story game for the browser, about a New
    Mexico *acequia madre*, by "acarey" on itch.io (about 2018)
    ([itch.io][walk]). Different medium and region; it uses the singular in a
    longer title.
  - *Acqueducts*: a 2-player tableau game on The Game Crafter by Daniel McKinley
    (20 June 2020), about aqueducts ([The Game Crafter][acqueducts]). Different word.
  - Aquädukt and Aqua Romana (above) are about aqueducts, not acequias.
- Verdict: **no name clash found**. A trademark search (INPI Argentina, EUIPO,
  USPTO) was out of scope and was not made.

## Conclusion: what is new in Acequias

No game found combines all of these, and no game found is set in Mendoza's
irrigated oasis:

1. **Theme**: Mendoza's *acequias* and *turnos de riego* feeding vineyards of
   Argentine varietals, with *cortes* (blends) as a scoring goal. Wine games use
   worker placement and never model water; water games are set in Cape Verde, Rome,
   Egypt or the Alps.
2. **Neutral canals with owner tokens** drawn from a shared face-up market and laid
   on an open table from a river strip, where water passes only where both openings
   meet.
3. **Vineyards planted on hex corners whose value is split in thirds** among the
   owners of the watered canals on the 3 hexes, whoever planted the vineyard.
   Catan also places on corners, but each piece takes from its hexes; in Acequias
   the vineyard is shared out to its neighbours. Wadi and Brass let one watered tile
   or location feed several pieces, but do not split a fixed value.
4. **Floodgate bribes from a mandatory action die**: a gate on any opening, paid as
   debt that is subtracted at the end, plus Release, Resow and Move faces.
5. **Varietal roulette and bodegas** (sets of 3 different varietals).

### Overlap to watch

- **Santiago** is the nearest game: irrigation canals, a "bribe" to get a canal
  placed, fields that dry out, and one crop area paying several players. Acequias
  differs: no auction, no overseer role, canals placed by players themselves, bribes
  paid as end-game debt for a die face, and fixed thirds instead of area x markers.
  Keep it that way: avoid adding an auction for canal placement, an overseer who
  takes the bribe money, or "dry fields become desert" wording.
- **Catan**: corner placement plus a hex blocker. Acequias' floodgate closes an edge
  of a canal, not a whole hex, and it is a paid choice, not a 7 on two dice. Avoid a
  "thief" figure that sits on a hex.
- **Aquädukt**: only watered buildings score, and ownerless canals can cut rivals
  off. Acequias' canals carry owner tokens and score their thirds, which keeps it
  apart.
- **Waterworks / Saboteur**: sabotage of a shared pipe or tunnel. Acequias'
  floodgate is close in spirit; keep it a priced die result, not a hand of attack
  cards.

## Limits and uncertain facts

- BoardGameGeek was not reachable, so games listed only there (small or
  self-published Argentine games in particular) may be missing.
- Santiago's year: the publisher's English rulebook file is dated 2004-2005, while
  game directories give 2003 for the first edition; 2003 is used.
- Barrage: designers, publisher and year come from retailer listings only; some list
  a 2020 edition. 2019 is used as the first release
- Wadi's year and publisher were not confirmed; the rulebook page does
  not state them.
- Vinhos' original 2010 publisher was not confirmed.
- No trademark search was made.
