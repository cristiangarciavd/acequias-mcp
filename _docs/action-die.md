# The action die - what each face means

The Rules v3 action die has six faces. The rules for each face are in the
[Rules v3 rules sheet](rules-v3.md#1-roll-the-action-die-mandatory); this note says what each face
stands for in Mendoza's irrigation and wine life, and which icon is printed on it.
The printed die is part of #113.

**Background.** Mendoza is a desert: almost all the water it uses comes from melting
snow and glaciers in the Andes ([Irrigación, Aquabook: los ríos mendocinos][rios]).
The water is shared through canals and *acequias*. Each local *Inspección de Cauce*
sets a *cuadro de turno* that tells every user when their water comes, with time in
proportion to their land ([Gobierno de Mendoza, Irrigación and the Inspecciones de
Cauce][turnos]). Under the Water Law the canal inspector checks the *compuertas*
(floodgates) and intakes, and the *tomero repartidor* hands out the water by the
schedule ([Ley de Aguas de Mendoza, arts. 189 bis and 225][ley]). Every irrigator must
have a regulation intake and *compuerta* and keep to their turn
([Irrigación, derechos y obligaciones][obligaciones]).

## Floodgate - icon: a gate with a small window (or a T)

The *compuerta* is the sluice gate that lets water into a canal or shuts it off.
Whoever controls the gate controls who gets water: a *tomero* or a neighbour
closing the gate at the right moment can leave someone's vineyard dry for a whole
turn. In the game the face costs a **bribe** (debt): you pay to have the gate closed
where it hurts a rival, or where it protects you.

## Release - icon: a crossed-out gate

The opposite deal: you pay the bribe to have a closed *compuerta* opened again and
taken away, so the water runs once more down the canal and to the vineyards that
depend on it.

## Resow - icon: a recycle arrow

A grower who replants a plot with another variety. Vineyards in Mendoza are regrafted
and replanted as tastes and markets change: in recent years Cabernet Franc plantings
grew fast while Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah shrank (see the
[varietal fact card](varietals.md)). In the game you spin the roulette again for any
planted vineyard, yours or a rival's.

## Move - icon: a moving truck

A grower who gives up a plot and starts again somewhere with better water: the
vineyard is moved to a new corner next to your own watered canal, and keeps its
varietal.

## Good season - icon: a water drop

A good winter of snow in the Andes means a good snowmelt (*deshielo*), full rivers
and canals, and time to do more: in the game, two actions this turn. Mendoza's
rivers are fed almost entirely by melting snow and glaciers
([Irrigación, Aquabook: los ríos mendocinos][rios]).

## Drought - icon: wind

A dry year, or a day of *Zonda*: a hot, dry wind
([Gobierno de Mendoza, recomendaciones por el viento Zonda][zonda2]) that exposes
Mendoza's farms to damage; almost half of the Zonda days recorded in one long study fell
in August and early September, around the time fruit trees and vines bloom
([Universidad Nacional de Cuyo, Revista de la Facultad de Ciencias Agrarias][zonda]).
Nothing gets done: you lose your action this turn.
