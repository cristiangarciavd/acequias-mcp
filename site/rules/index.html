A game of water and vineyards from Mendoza

Acequias

Bring the river's water through your canals, plant vineyards on their corners, put together your cortes and build your bodega before the vineyards run out. But watch out: a well-placed floodgate can leave half a farm dry.

Players2 to 5 (Flash: 2 or 3) Length15 to 30 min (Flash: 10 to 20) Age8+

⚠ Warning: contains small parts. Not suitable for children under 3.

AcequiasComponents

Components

The river strip

A base segment for 2 players and one segment that clips on for each extra player. Each segment has 5 river hexes. The rest of the table stays open: no stones and no printed plots.

Canal pieces (neutral)

Six shapes, drawn from the bag: 16 pieces per player. The bag holds about 44% straights, 22% gentle curves, 17% sharp curves, 6% Y, 6% T and 5% claws.

AcequiasSetup and turn

Goal

Build canals that carry water from the river, plant vineyards next to them and have more points than anyone when the game ends.

Setup

  1. River. Build the river strip from the base segment and one more segment for each player after the second. All water comes from the river.
  2. Bag. Put 16 canals per player in the bag and shuffle them. The bag holds no floodgates.
  3. Cluster supply. Build the shared supply: 2 clusters of each varietal with two sectors on the roulette and 1 Bonarda and 1 Cabernet Franc (14 in all). In Normal with 4 or 5 players use 3 of each and 1 Bonarda and Cabernet Franc (20 in all).
  4. Market. Draw 4 pieces and lay them face up next to the river: this is the market.
  5. Players. Each player takes their owner pegs and their bodega token. The floodgates, the debt tokens, the roulette and the die go in the middle.
  6. Start player. Choose who starts. Each round the next player starts, like the turnos de riego (watering turns) of Mendoza's acequias.

The turn

Each round everyone plays one turn, in order. A turn has two steps: 1. roll the die (always) and 2. take your action.

1 · Roll the die and do what it shows

The floodgate bribe costs 2 debt in Normal and 1 in Flash. You pay it only if you place or remove the floodgate: if you do not use it, it costs nothing.

2 · Take your action

Unless you rolled a drought, you take one action (two with a good season):

  • Place a canal. Take a piece from the market and put it on an empty hex, with your peg. At least one of its openings must face the river or the opening of a canal already on the table, even a dry one or one closed by a floodgate. Its other openings may face anything. Refill the market from the bag; when the bag is empty, the market shrinks.
  • Plant a vineyard (see "Planting").
  • Build your bodega (see "Bodega"). Once per game.
  • Pass. Always allowed.
AcequiasWater and planting

Water

  • Water enters a canal through an opening that faces the river.
  • It passes from one canal to its neighbour only where both have an opening facing each other.
  • Inside a piece, water reaches all of its openings.
  • A floodgate closes one opening: water does not cross that edge in either direction. If it closes the entrance, it dries that piece and everything downstream. If it closes an exit, it cuts only that way. Only one floodgate fits on each edge.

Red takes water from the river and passes it to its second piece through the facing openings. Green's straight is dried by a floodgate on its intake.

Planting

  • Vineyards do not go on hexes: they go on corners, where 3 hexes meet.
  • You may plant on a free corner that touches one of your canals with water. The corner touches the canal if the canal is on one of its 3 hexes, whichever way the openings point.
  • The other two hexes may be anything: rivals' canals, the river or empty table.
  • Spin the roulette: take a cluster of that varietal from the supply and put it there with your peg on top. The vineyard is yours. Once the supply is empty, no one can plant.
  • Respins. If the roulette lands on a varietal with no clusters left in the supply, spin again, up to 3 spins in all. On a resow the old cluster goes back to the supply first, so its own varietal always works.
  • Lost turn. If all 3 spins fail you plant nothing (a failed resow keeps its varietal) and you lose the rest of your turn. It counts as a lost turn only if you did not plant or resow anything earlier that turn.

The 6 corners of one of your canals with water are places to plant.

AcequiasScoring and end

A vineyard is split in thirds

A vineyard is worth 6, or 9 if it is Bonarda or Cabernet Franc. Its value is split into three thirds, one for each hex around its corner:

  • Each third goes to the owner of the canal on that hex, only if that canal has water at the end. It does not matter who planted the vineyard.
  • A third on the river, on an empty hex or on a dry canal scores for nobody.

Cortes (blends)

You hold a varietal if you score at least one watered third of a vineyard of that varietal, whoever planted it, as it is at the end. Each corte whose varietals you all hold scores once. The same vineyard may count for several cortes.

Bodega

Once per game you may use an action to build your bodega: put your bodega token on a free corner where you could plant. You must have planted 2 different reds and 1 white (Torrontés or Chardonnay). The bodega is worth 12 and is split in thirds like a vineyard: 4 for each watered third, for the owner of each canal. It has no varietal and is never moved or resown.

End of the game

The game ends with the first of these three things to happen:

  • Supply empty: someone takes the last cluster. The round is played out.
  • Score target: someone reaches the target score. The round is played out.
  • Dead turns: every player has lost 2 turns in a row because their spins failed. The game ends at once.

With 2 players and in Flash the game usually ends by the score target; with 3 to 5 in Normal, because the clusters run out. Flash is the short game, for 2 or 3 players, with the same rules and a lower target.

Score = watered thirds + cortes + bodega − debt

The highest score wins. On a tie, the player with more watered vineyards (vineyards with at least one watered third of theirs) wins. If they are still tied, they share the win.

AcequiasThe die and its history

Why the die has these faces

Mendoza is a desert: almost all the water it uses comes from melting snow and glaciers in the Andes1. The water is shared out through canals and acequias. Each Inspección de Cauce draws up a cuadro de turno (turn schedule) that tells each irrigator when their water arrives, for a time in proportion to their land2. Under the Ley de Aguas (Water Law), the canal inspector controls the floodgates and intakes, and the tomero repartidor delivers the water by that schedule3. Each irrigator must have their own intake and floodgate, and respect their turn4.

AcequiasThe varietals

The eight varietals

The roulette has 14 equal sectors. Bonarda and Cabernet Franc have one sector each and are worth 9; the others have two and are worth 6. The clusters are printed in three colors: violet, red and fluorescent green for the whites. The abbreviation on the side of the cluster tells you which varietal it is. Areas are hectares of vineyard at 31 December 2025, according to the INV.
